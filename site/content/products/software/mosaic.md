## What MOSAIC is
I build frameworks like this to turn ad hoc, one-off comparison scripts into a repeatable process. MOSAIC automates pairing ocean model output with observational data to compute standard skill metrics, flag periods and regions of poor agreement, and generate calibration diagnostics.

## Compatible data sources
- Buoys
- Tide/water-level gauges
- Satellite
- ADCP

## What MOSAIC can do
- Automated pairing of model output with observational data across stations and time periods
- Computes standard skill metrics
- Flags periods/regions of poor model-observation agreement
- Generates calibration diagnostics to guide parameter tuning

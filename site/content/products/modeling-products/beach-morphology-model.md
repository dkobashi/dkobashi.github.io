## Description ##

[XBeach](https://www.deltares.nl/en/software-and-data/products/xbeach) is a process-based model for storm-driven beach and dune morphodynamics — simulating wave, current, and sediment-transport processes at the event scale to predict how a beach and dune system erodes during a storm. I run it nested inside a regional storm-surge and wave hindcast or forecast (e.g. SCHISM-WWM), which supplies the offshore water-level and wave boundary conditions the local XBeach domain needs.

- Storm-driven beach and dune erosion simulation, event by event
- Nested regional-to-local modeling: a regional storm hindcast/forecast feeding the high-resolution XBeach domain
- Validation against pre- and post-storm topographic surveys (e.g. airborne lidar)
- Shoreline and dune-erosion risk assessment for coastal engineering and resilience planning

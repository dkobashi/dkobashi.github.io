date: 2026-01-01
description: "Delivered defensible design-basis metocean criteria for an offshore structure in the Gulf of Mexico, used directly in engineering design."
challenge: "The client needed site-specific design values for waves, currents, wind, and water levels, but the available on-site observational record was too short to characterize extreme conditions directly."
approach: "Combined a long-term hindcast/reanalysis dataset with extreme-value analysis (peaks-over-threshold, multiple return periods) and validated the results against available buoy and gauge observations."
result: "Delivered a design-basis report with multi-return-period criteria that the client incorporated directly into structural design and permitting."

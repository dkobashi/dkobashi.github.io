---
title: "Fine Sediment Dynamics"
date: 2026-01-01
description: "Analyzed storm-driven dispersal of fluvial fine sediment on a low-energy inner shelf and its influence on an offshore sand shoal, using in-situ measurements and satellite imagery."
challenge: "The Louisiana coast is a low-energy, microtidal shelf whose sediment is predominantly fine (silt/clay) due to influx from the Mississippi and Atchafalaya rivers. It was unclear why and how this fine sediment persists on an otherwise sandy shoal, how its dynamics differ from sand, and whether it would affect future sand-mining operations there."
approach: "Analyzed prolonged in-situ measurements and satellite imagery across winter storms and tropical cyclones to characterize storm-driven sediment resuspension and offshore advection pathways."
result: "Published in *Regional Studies in Marine Science* (Kobashi & Jose, 2019) — found storm-driven advection transports resuspended fine sediment from the bay to the inner shelf and shoal regardless of storm type, with the dispersal mechanism varying by storm type, intensity, and track."
